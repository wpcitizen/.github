# WP Citizen brand basics

## Logo assets

The approved horizontal logo is stored in [`profile/assets/`](profile/assets/):

- `wp-citizen-final.svg` — preferred scalable vector artwork.
- `wp-citizen-final.png` — transparent raster export.
- `wp-citizen-final-plate.svg` — the logo on a white plate, for pages that may show a dark background, such as the GitHub profile.
- `wp-citizen-avatar.svg` / `wp-citizen-avatar.png` — square WP emblem crop for GitHub organization avatar use.

Use the artwork as supplied. Preserve proportions, spacing, and the circular geometry. Do not add gradients, shadows, outlines around the lettering, or extra elements. Do not stretch, recolor, or place the transparent logo on a background that makes the navy wordmark unreadable.

## Core colors

| Use | Hex | Sample |
|---|---|---|
| WP lettering | `#1269E8` | Bright blue |
| Citizen lettering | `#073878` | Deep navy |
| Outer circle | `#176FF2` | Strong blue |
| Inner circle | `#78A7F5` | Light blue |

The logo uses four flat colors. For supporting UI, use the core colors as accents and verify text/background contrast independently.

## Typography

The logo lettering is converted to vector paths. For supporting brand materials, use a clean sans-serif such as Inter or a system sans-serif stack. Keep headings clear and compact; prioritize legibility over decorative styling.

## Voice

Be practical, clear, helpful, technically accurate, and respectful. Explain benefits in terms of real workflows. Avoid inflated claims, unverified adoption statistics, and vague superlatives.
