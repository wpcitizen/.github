# Community Code of Conduct

WP Citizen welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and perspectives. We are committed to making our project spaces respectful, constructive, and safe for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment include:

- Being considerate, respectful, and collaborative.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing criticism on ideas, code, and decisions rather than people.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Taking responsibility, correcting mistakes, and learning from them.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and sustained disruption.
- Sexualized language or imagery in community spaces.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Threats, doxxing, or encouraging others to target someone.
- Deliberately derailing discussions or repeatedly ignoring moderator guidance.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in WP Citizen organization repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, project spaces, and when someone is representing a WP Citizen project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainers at [dev@mizandev.com](mailto:dev@mizandev.com). Do not post sensitive details in a public issue or discussion.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, respectfully, and as confidentially as practical. They may remove content, edit or close discussions, temporarily restrict participation, or remove a participant from a project space when necessary. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), adapted for WP Citizen project spaces.
